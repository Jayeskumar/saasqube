The craft

Twenty-one days, one saree.

01
01 — SILK

Pure mulberry silk

We source thick, lustrous mulberry silk yarn and dye it in small batches, so every colour is rich and even.

02 — LOOM

Traditional pit looms

Each saree is set up on a wooden handloom by our master weavers, the same way it has been done in Kanchipuram for centuries.

03 — KORVAI

The korvai join

Body and border are woven separately and interlocked by hand, so the contrast border never fades or separates.

04 — ZARI

Real silver zari

Our zari is silver thread dipped in gold. It's tested for purity and comes with a certificate for every bridal saree.
